Why Vastram

Price, quality, colours

Wear tradition, live beautifully.

  • Directly from weavers

    Authentic handwoven sarees straight from the looms of India. No middlemen, fair prices for the artisans.

  • Stitched blouse included

    Most sarees come with a ready-stitched blouse, with customised options too. Prefer one without, for a temple offering? We have those as well.

  • Premium silks

    Kanjeevaram, soft silk, semi silk, Banarasi and more, chosen for weave, colour and quality.

  • For every occasion and budget

    Weddings, festivals, poojas and everyday elegance, with sarees starting at $49.99.

  • Pre-orders for weddings

    Tell us what you need. We'll source it from our weavers and deliver it on time.

  • Personal service

    Shop with us on WhatsApp, pick up in Johns Creek, GA, or get it shipped anywhere in the US.
