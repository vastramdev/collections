Our story

We work directly with weavers in India to bring their sarees to you — no middlemen, just beautiful handwork and fair prices for the artisans who make it.

What to expect

The Vastram way

  • Straight from India

    Every saree is imported directly from India and chosen with care for its weave, colour and drape.

  • Ready to wear

    Each saree comes with a stitched blouse, so you can drape it as soon as it arrives.

  • Local & personal

    Pick up in Johns Creek, GA, or meet us at a pop-up stall to see the fabrics in person.
