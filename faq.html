Help

Frequently asked questions

Ordering & shipping

Where do you ship?

We currently ship within the United States only.

How much is shipping, and how long does it take?

Shipping is a flat $15.99 per order.

Orders usually arrive within 3–5 business days.

How do I pay online?

All online orders go through our secure Square checkout, where you can pay by card. Your card details never touch this website.

What is your return policy?

All sales are final — we don't accept returns or exchanges. If you have any questions about a saree, please message us before you order and we'll gladly send more photos or details.

The sarees

Does the saree come with a blouse?

Yes. Every saree comes with a stitched blouse, so it's ready to wear.

Sizing details are listed with each saree in our online store. Not sure about fit? Message us before you order.

Will the colour look exactly like the photo?

We work to show true colours in our photos, but screens vary, and handwoven fabrics can show small irregularities that are part of their character. If you'd like more photos or a video, just ask.

How do I care for my saree?

See our care & draping guide. In short, dry clean silks and anything with zari.

Stalls & pickup

How can I pay at your stalls and pop-ups?

We accept:

  • Card: credit and debit
  • Zelle
  • Cash
Where can I see the sarees in person?

Check upcoming pop-ups & stalls on our home page, or follow us on Instagram for dates.

Do you offer local pickup?

Yes, local pickup is available in Johns Creek, GA. Message us to arrange a time. We'll share the exact pickup details after you order.

Still have a question? Contact us. We're happy to help.
